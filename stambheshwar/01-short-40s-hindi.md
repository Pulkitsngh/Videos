## The hook (0–4 s): what stops the scroll

> **"ये मंदिर रोज़ दो बार समुद्र में डूब जाता है…"** *(beat)* **"…और फिर लौट आता है!"**

- **The visual comes first.** At frame 1, the wave is already crashing over the temple's spire. No logo, no intro, no "नमस्कार".
- **Why this line works.**
  - It makes an impossible-sounding claim in 7 words.
  - "रोज़ दो बार" is a precise detail, so it sounds real, not like a legend.
  - The pause after "डूब जाता है" leaves an open question that makes people stay.
- **On screen at 0:00, big and yellow:** **रोज़ 2 बार गायब!**

**Backup hooks** (same visual, for A/B testing across uploads):
1. "इस मंदिर में दर्शन का समय पुजारी नहीं… समुद्र तय करता है।"
2. "भारत का वो मंदिर जो दिन में दो बार पानी में छिप जाता है।"

## Fact notes (check before posting)

- Location and the twice-daily submergence at high tide: ✔ widely documented. It's a real tidal effect in the Gulf of Khambhat.
- The Kartikeya / Tarakasura origin comes from the Skanda Purana tradition. It's presented as **"कहा जाता है"**, a belief, not a historical fact.
- Visitors are commonly given tide timings. Phrase this as common practice, not an official rule.
- "वडोदरा से लगभग 75–80 किमी" in the description is an approximate road distance. Adjust it or remove the line if you prefer.
