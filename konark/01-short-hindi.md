## Fact notes (check before posting)

- **Location and distances:** Konark, Puri district, Odisha, on the Bay of Bengal coast. About 35 km from Puri, about 65 km from Bhubaneswar ✔ (approximate road distances).
- **Builder and date:** King **Narasimhadeva I** of the Eastern Ganga dynasty, **c. 1250 CE** ✔.
- **Design:** the Sun god's chariot, with **24 wheels (12 pairs)** and **7 horses** ✔.
- **Wheels as sundials:** widely cited ✔. Each wheel (~2.7 m) has 8 major and 8 minor spokes that can be read like a sundial. The script says "लगभग सही समय" rather than claiming minute-exact accuracy.
- **Magnet, floating idol and ship compasses:** **legend / folklore**, labelled as किंवदंती. No archaeological proof.
- **"Black Pagoda":** a name used by European sailors as a coastal landmark ✔. It's in the description only.
- **Collapse:** the main sanctum tower (deul) collapsed centuries ago. The exact cause and date are debated (structural failure, foundation problems and so on). The magnet explanation is legend.
- **1903 sand-filling:** the jagamohana (assembly hall) was filled with sand and sealed in **1903** under British-era conservation to prevent its collapse ✔. The ASI has since studied removing the sand. The script doesn't say what's inside or claim anything about current status.
- **₹10 note:** the RBI's 2018 ₹10 banknote shows the Konark Sun Temple (wheel motif) on the reverse ✔.
- **UNESCO:** inscribed in **1984** ✔.
