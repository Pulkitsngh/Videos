# World Unscripted: 10-second channel intro (character-narrated)

**Channel:** World Unscripted · **Language:** Hindi (English and Hinglish alternates below)
**Host:** your Higgsfield character, on camera and speaking the lines
**Format:** make both 16:9 (long videos) and 9:16 (Shorts). The script works for both.

**Tagline:** **"दुनिया की वो कहानियाँ, जो किसी स्क्रिप्ट में नहीं लिखी गईं।"**
This plays on the channel name: real mysteries, real history, nothing scripted by anyone.

Replace **[नाम]** with your character's name everywhere.

---

## Script A: main intro (Hindi, ~10 s) ✅ recommended

| Time | Shot | Character says (on camera / VO) | On-screen text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:02** | Darkness. A lantern flickers on and lights the character's face. They look straight into the lens. | "कुछ कहानियाँ… कोई नहीं लिखता।" | — | A match strikes, then a low drone swells |
| **0:02–0:06** | Rapid montage, ~1 s each: the wave swallowing the sea temple, cloth sliding under the hanging pillar, the shell in the desert sand, then back to the character walking through a stone corridor | (VO) "वो बस… घट जाती हैं। रहस्य… इतिहास… चमत्कार…" | रहस्य · इतिहास · चमत्कार (one word per flash) | A whoosh on each cut, a heartbeat-style hit on "चमत्कार" |
| **0:06–0:10** | The character stops, turns to camera with a slight smile, and the lantern light flares. The logo builds behind them. | "मैं हूँ [नाम]… और ये है **World Unscripted**।" | **WORLD UNSCRIPTED**, and under it: दुनिया, बिना स्क्रिप्ट के। | A deep "boom" on the logo, then silence |

**Spoken words:** ~22, which fits 10 s with dramatic pauses.

**Montage footage:** reuse your existing Short clips (Stambheshwar wave, Lepakshi cloth, Tanot shell). That keeps the look consistent and costs no extra credits.

## Script B: English version (for an English channel trailer or international Shorts)

- **2–6 s:** "They just… happen. Mysteries. History. Miracles."
- **6–10 s:** "I'm [Name]… and this is **World Unscripted**."

- **0–2 s:** "Kuch stories… koi script nahi karta."
- **2–6 s:** "Wo bas… ho jaati hain. Mysteries, history, miracles…"
- **6–10 s:** "Main hoon [Naam]… aur ye hai **World Unscripted**!"

## Script D: 4-second bumper (to open every Short without losing viewers)

Use it at the **end** of Shorts, not the start: the first 4 seconds of a Short must be the hook.

> Character, lantern in hand: "रहस्य और भी हैं… देखते रहिए **World Unscripted**।" + logo sting

## Character direction (for your Higgsfield character)

- **Look:** a curious explorer-storyteller. Earthy jacket or kurta layered with a shawl, canvas satchel, an old brass **lantern** as the signature prop (use it in every video for branding).
- **Performance:** calm, low voice. Every line starts almost as a whisper and lands with confidence on the channel name. Give a slight head tilt and a knowing half-smile on "World Unscripted".
- **Lighting:** warm lantern key light on the face, deep shadows, golden dust in the air. It matches the Shorts' amber and teal palette.
- **Setting:** an ancient stone corridor or temple doorway at night. It isn't a specific real temple, so it works for any topic.

## Production notes (Higgsfield)

1. **Voice:** generate the lines with the **same ElevenLabs "Gideon" Hindi voice** you've used in the Shorts, so the channel has one recognisable voice. Or pick a different voice for the character if you want the host to sound distinct from the documentary narrator.
2. **Character shots (0–2 s and 6–10 s):** use **Seedance 2.0** with your character image as the image reference and the voice line as the audio reference, so the lips sync to the line. Two clips of about 5 s at 9:16 and 16:9.
   - *Prompt, shot 1:* "[character] in a dark ancient stone corridor, lights an old brass lantern, the warm flame reveals their face, they look directly into camera and speak softly, golden dust in the air, cinematic, shallow depth of field."
   - *Prompt, shot 3:* "[character] walking through a torch-lit stone temple corridor stops, turns to camera with a subtle knowing smile and speaks, the lantern glow flares brighter, cinematic, warm amber and teal."
3. **Montage (2–6 s):** 1-second cuts from the existing Tanot, Stambheshwar and Lepakshi clips.
4. **Logo:** gold serif "WORLD UNSCRIPTED" with a faint map texture, revealed with a light sweep from the lantern flare.
5. **Music:** the same original tanpura drone as the Lepakshi Short, with a boom hit on the logo, so the brand has a sonic signature.

**Rough cost:** 2 × 5 s Seedance 2.0 clips at 720p ≈ 45 credits, 1080p ≈ 90 credits, plus ~1 credit of voice. The montage is free (reused clips).
