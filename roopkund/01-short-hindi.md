## Fact notes (check before posting)

- **Location:** Chamoli district, Uttarakhand, Trishul massif, ~5,029 m (16,500 ft) ✔. It's a glacial lake, frozen for much of the year ✔.
- **Discovery:** remains widely reported after **1942** by a Nanda Devi game-reserve ranger (usually named **H. K. Madhwal**) ✔.
- **Number of people:** estimates vary (a few hundred, up to ~500 or more is commonly quoted), so the script says "सैकड़ों".
- **Hail theory:** a 2004 study reported skull injuries consistent with blows from large round objects and suggested a severe hailstorm around the 9th century. It's presented as **"एक थ्योरी"**.
- **2019 DNA study** (*Nature Communications*, 38 individuals): at least three genetically distinct groups.
  - A **South Asian** group dated to about **7th–10th century CE**
  - An **eastern-Mediterranean-related** group (closest to present-day Greece/Crete) dated to about **1800 CE**
  - One individual with **East Asian**-related ancestry
  - So "कुछ लोग भारत के थे ही नहीं" and "~1000 साल बाद" are accurate simplifications.
- **Nanda Devi's curse** is a **legend**, presented as लोककथा. The Nanda Devi Raj Jat pilgrimage passes near Roopkund, roughly every 12 years.
- **Trek access:** camping and trek permissions in the region's alpine meadows (bugyals) have been restricted at times, so viewers should check current rules. That's why the description only says how the lake is reached, not that it's open.
