## What happened (summary of reporting as of 30 Sep / 1 Oct 2026)

- **Flight:** **flydubai FZ1073**, **Dubai → Tel Aviv**, Boeing 737 MAX 8, **174 people on board** (some outlets say ~180).
- **In the cockpit:** the co-pilot, reported to be an **Omani national**, **allegedly stabbed the captain** and tried to crash the plane. The jet dropped **thousands of feet within seconds**, and the hijack code was triggered.
- **The heroes:**
  - **Captain Smit Machchhar (कैप्टन स्मित मच्छर):** Indian, from Mumbai, ex-SpiceJet, about 9,750 flying hours. Despite being stabbed and seriously injured, he fought back and **opened the cockpit door**.
  - **Yaniv Chayon (यानिव चायोन):** passenger, a plumber travelling with his family. He rushed into the cockpit, **pulled the attacker off the controls** and pulled the control column back.
  - **Assaf Rajuan (असफ़ राजुआन):** passenger who helped **overpower the attacker** with other passengers and the crew.
  - **Dr. Shota Musev (डॉ. शोता मुसेव):** passenger, a dentist. He **tied up the attacker** with headphone cords and zip ties, then **treated the wounded captain**.
  - **Two off-duty airline pilots** travelling as passengers (reported as South African and New Zealand citizens; **names not published**), with the **cabin crew**. They took control, levelled the plane and **landed it safely at Tabuk, Saudi Arabia**.
- **Afterwards:** the co-pilot was detained. **The motive is unknown and under formal investigation.** flydubai **temporarily suspended flights to and from Israel**.
