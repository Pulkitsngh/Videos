## Fact notes (check before posting)

- Veerabhadra Temple, Lepakshi: built in the 1530s by the brothers Virupanna and Viranna under Vijayanagara king Achyuta Deva Raya ✔. "500 साल" is a fair round figure.
- About 70 pillars, one of which does not fully rest on the floor ✔. Visitors passing cloth under it is a real, common practice.
- The British engineer who tried to move the pillar is **folklore**. It's often attributed to an engineer in the early 1900s, but it isn't well documented, so it's kept as "कहा जाता है".
- The engineering explanation (the pillar slipped slightly, and the load is shared by the other pillars) is the commonly cited view.
- The "ले पक्षी" / Jatayu story of the name is a **legend**. It's only in the description, and labelled as मान्यता.
- The distance from Bengaluru (~120 km) is approximate.
