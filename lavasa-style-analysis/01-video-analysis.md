## 4. Attractions and thrills (retention devices)

**Mid-video re-hooks.** A new "turn" arrives roughly every 60–90 seconds:
- 4:12 "*Clear skies don't last forever*" (a foreshadowing proverb)
- 4:19 "*In 2010, the project hit a major roadblock*" (red-tinted 3D model)
- 5:14 "*…but that wasn't the whole story*" (an escalation phrase)
- 6:19 "*As months turned into years… more details came to light*"
- 7:50 "*Investigations deepened…*"
- 8:29 "*Amidst the growing political and legal storm…*"

**Escalation ladder.** Each problem is bigger and more human than the last: **Environment → Legal → Political/money → Human → Financial collapse.** The viewer keeps watching because every layer raises the stakes.

**Emotional devices**
- **Stakes for ordinary people:** "*people invested their life savings and took out loans*" (3:30), planted early and paid off at 10:07 with the crying buyer
- **Villain ambiguity:** never names a villain directly. It's always "allegations" and "questions were raised", so the viewer draws the conclusion (safe legally and engaging)
- **Irony ending:** a city meant to rival Portofino now charges an entry fee for pre-wedding shoots (10:31–11:19). This is the most shareable moment
- **Colour storytelling:** full colour for the dream, **red tint** for crisis, **black-and-white** for ruin (10:00, 10:39, 12:12)

**Visual thrills (motion-graphics vocabulary)**
- 3D fly-through of the master plan with labelled pins (hotels, convention centre, golf)
- Rubber-stamp slams: `COMPLAIN`, `SOLD`, `BANKRUPT`, 🚫
- Emblem vs. emblem: state vs. central government logos with ✅ / ❌
- Cartoon reaction inserts: a confused man, a crying businessman, a confused cat, a laughing man
- Floating currency notes over the lake (money draining away)
- A "Past vs. Present" split card
- A magnifying glass over the hill graphic ("investigation")
- A calendar flipping over the city (time passing)

**Engagement CTA sequence (final 45 s):** a comment prompt, a one-line moral, like (cursor click), subscribe + bell (cursor click), and a patriotic sign-off.
