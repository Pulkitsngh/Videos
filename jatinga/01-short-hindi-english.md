## Why the first 5 seconds work
1. **Frame 1 is the strangest image:** birds diving out of the fog towards a light at night. The scroll stops on it.
2. **The first line is impossible-sounding:** "birds fall from the sky".
3. **A second shock at 0:03:** "they come here to die". Viewers stay for the explanation, which only arrives at 0:17.
4. **The place name arrives only at 0:06,** after the viewer is already hooked.

## Facts and wording (keep it accurate and kind)
- Jatinga is in **Dima Hasao district, Assam**, near Haflong.
- The phenomenon is reported mainly **Sept–Nov**, on **dark, moonless, misty or foggy nights**, usually in the **evening (about 6–10 pm)**.
- Birds (many of them local and juvenile birds) fly towards lights.
- **"Bird suicide" is a myth.** Ornithologists (the area was studied and publicised from the 1950s–60s; Salim Ali is often mentioned) explain it as **disorientation from weather plus attraction to light**.
- In the past, birds that came down were **caught or killed by people**. Awareness and conservation efforts have since reduced this, and there are tourism and bird-watching events.
- **Don't show dead or injured birds or killing.** Keep it mysterious, not gory. YouTube may age-restrict or limit animal-harm content.
- Say "हज़ारों/सैकड़ों पक्षी" loosely. Exact numbers vary by year and source.
